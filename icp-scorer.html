Why this exists

Fit is structural, intent is observable

Fit tells you whether an account can ever be a good customer. Intent tells you whether it can be one now. Confusing the two is how sellers spend a quarter on accounts that were never going to move.

The mid-market is scored highest on purpose

The largest accounts fit, but their cycles run longest and their committees run widest. The beachhead is the tier that can decide in a quarter.

The stop is not a feature

The jurisdiction disqualifier overrides every score, quietly. In a regulated business, knowing when not to sell is part of knowing how to sell.
