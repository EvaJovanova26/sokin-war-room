The Weekend Test

"Pix never sleeps. Your dollar leg does."

Brazil pays instantly, around the clock. The dollar side of the same payment stops at Friday 5PM and waits for Monday. This campaign is built entirely on that gap: one corridor, three phases in sequence, and a stated point at which each phase stops if the evidence says so.

Wedge

Phase 1 · weeks 1 to 6

Focus entirely on the Friday to Monday gap in the US to Brazil corridor. The entry motion is a weekend liquidity audit: the cost of waiting model, run against the prospect's own volumes, timed to land in the week before month-end when the pain is live.

Stops if

Fewer than ten qualified accounts agree to run the audit by the end of week 6. If the corridor's treasurers will not even look at their own weekend numbers, the wedge is wrong or the corridor is wrong, and the campaign moves to the next candidate corridor rather than pushing harder on this one.

Ground level

The sequenceFive touches over three weeks, all anchored to the calendar: the first lands the Tuesday before month-end, the last lands the Monday after, referencing the weekend that just happened.

The talk track"What happened to your Brazil payouts last Friday at 5PM? We can put a dollar figure on it in twenty minutes, using your numbers, not ours."

The assetThe cost of waiting calculator, pre-set to the corridor, sent as a link the controller can drag around before anyone asks for a meeting.

Proof

Phase 2 · weeks 7 to 14

Three design partners drawn from the existing customer book, not new logos. A time-boxed pilot in the corridor where the deliverable being sold is the settlement data the pilot generates: their own before and after, documented. The platform is the means, the evidence is the product.

Stops if

Fewer than two design partners are signed and in onboarding by the end of week 10, or the pilot data shows no material difference against the partners' own baseline by week 14. Evidence that does not exist cannot be scaled, and manufacturing it retroactively is how challenger brands destroy trust.

Ground level

The sequenceDirect outreach from the account owner, not marketing, to the five best-fit existing accounts with corridor exposure. Design partner terms in the first conversation, not the third.

The talk track"You already trust us with part of your flow. Run the Brazil weekend leg with us for eight weeks. You keep the settlement data either way, and it is yours to beat your bank up with even if you never expand."

The assetA one-page pilot charter: scope, duration, what gets measured, who sees the data, and the exit terms. Reviewed by compliance before the first partner sees it.

Scale

Phase 3 · week 15 onward

Take the pilot data to ERP and accounting platform partners active in the corridor, and open an embedded route alongside direct sales. The partner conversation starts from documented settlement evidence, which is the only opening a platform partnerships team takes seriously.

Stops if

No platform partner has agreed to a scoping conversation within eight weeks of the pilot data being available. That would mean the evidence persuades treasurers but not platforms, in which case the corridor motion continues direct-only and the partner route is re-examined with different evidence, not louder outreach.

Ground level

The sequenceMap which ERPs the design partners themselves run. Warm introduction through the shared customer beats a cold partnerships deck every time.

The talk track"Your mutual customer cut their weekend settlement gap in this corridor. Here is their documented before and after. There are more of them on your platform."

The assetA co-brandable corridor report built from the pilot data, plus an embedded Send integration brief for the partner's product team.

Why this exists

The position this page argues: a bet without a stopping rule is a plan with confident adjectives. Every phase above names the evidence, and the date, that kills it. That is what separates a campaign from a list of activities, and it is the discipline that lets a small team place a concentrated bet without betting the year.

Why one corridor

Concentration is the whole strategy. A challenger spread across every corridor is invisible in all of them. One corridor, won visibly, produces the evidence that opens the next.

What this does not claim

Nothing here forces an incumbent to react. A corridor beachhead does not move a global bank, and pretending otherwise would be the one junior note in the plan. The beachhead stands on its own economics.
