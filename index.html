Why this exists

Most ROI calculators compare two spreads and declare victory. Finance teams do not believe them, because the spread is the one number they already know. The persuadable cost sits in the three lines nobody prices: fees per wire, hours per exception, capital per day in transit.

The deliberate choices

Chasing hours default to zero so the prospect claims the pain rather than being told they have it. Sokin's rate is never guessed; the model asks for the published rate rather than fabricating one. The challenger column is not zeros, because a residual is what makes the total survive a CFO's first question.

Where it sits

Sent ahead of a first call so the conversation starts from the prospect's own numbers, then re-run live with their controller in the room.
